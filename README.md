## 👋 About Me

I am a **Software Engineering graduate** who enjoys working where **software meets physical hardware**. I like understanding how a system works end to end, from the signal on a wire or the state of a controller, up to the data pipeline, service or dashboard that depends on it.

My work covers **circuit design & simulation, PLC programming, embedded systems, backend and data systems, and Linux-based tooling**. I approach problems systematically: reproduce, isolate, measure, fix, document.
